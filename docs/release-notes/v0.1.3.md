# mai2srt v0.1.3

修复转录因账号未接受「生物特征通知」而被 HTTP 451 拒绝的问题（[#1](https://github.com/fuxiaomoke/mai2srt/issues/1)）：playground 把音频视为生物特征数据（BIPA），账号须先接受一次通知才能上传音频。本次更新解释了这个报错并给出明确的指引，在桌面端与命令行各加了一个接受入口，并让新账号在登录时就完成检测——已经接受过的账号不受任何影响。

## 生物特征通知

- playground 把音频视为生物特征数据（BIPA）：账号需要先接受一次「生物特征通知」才能上传音频。网页端靠首次上传时弹出的同意框完成这一步，而本工具走无头通道看不到弹窗，所以需要自己的接受入口。
- 新账号登录完成时会自动检测：需要接受的话，设置页的「接受生物特征通知」按钮会高亮并给出提示，点一次即可。
- 桌面端：**设置 → 文件与账号 → 「接受生物特征通知」**；已经撞上 451 的话，转录错误信息也会直接指引到这里。
- 命令行：新账号 `mai2srt login` 完成后会当场询问，确认即接受；转录遇到 451 时也会当场询问，确认后自动接受并继续，无需重跑。也可以随时运行 `mai2srt consent`。
- 接受记录保存在微软账号上，一次生效、长期有效，之后转录正常进行。
- 本工具不会替你静默接受：这是法律意义上的同意，确认永远由你本人做出。多账号用户请注意，每个账号需要各自接受一次。

## 安装

下载下面的 `mai2srt_0.1.3_x64-setup.exe` 安装，FFmpeg 已随包内置。可以直接覆盖安装。

首次安装可能出现 Windows SmartScreen 蓝窗（安装包未签名）→ 点「更多信息」→「仍要运行」。附件中的 `SHA256SUMS.txt` 可用于校验下载是否完整。

## English

Fixes transcriptions being rejected with HTTP 451 when the account has not yet accepted the biometric notice ([#1](https://github.com/fuxiaomoke/mai2srt/issues/1)): playground treats audio as biometric data (BIPA), and each account must accept the notice once before audio uploads. This release explains the error and provides clear guidance, adds an acceptance entry point in both the GUI and the CLI, and checks fresh accounts at sign-in — accounts that have already accepted are unaffected.

## Biometric notice

- playground treats audio as biometric data (BIPA): an account must accept the "biometric notice" once before uploading audio. The website handles that with a consent dialog on the first upload; mai2srt's headless channel never sees the dialog, so the acceptance needs its own entry points here.
- A fresh sign-in checks automatically: when the notice is still owed, the "accept biometric notice" button in Settings is highlighted with a hint — one click and it is done.
- GUI: **Settings → Files & accounts → "accept biometric notice"**; if a run hits the 451, the transcription error itself points there.
- CLI: `mai2srt login` on a fresh account asks right after sign-in — confirm once and it is done; a transcription that hits the 451 asks on the spot too, and continues without a restart after confirming. `mai2srt consent` works at any time.
- The acceptance is stored on your Microsoft account, once and for all; transcription works normally afterwards.
- mai2srt never accepts silently on your behalf: this is a legal consent, and the confirmation is always yours to make. With multiple accounts, each account needs its own one-time acceptance.
